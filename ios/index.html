CubeFlick privacy policy (iPhone and iPad)

Last updated: 12 November 2026. Applies to CubeFlick: Arrow Escape 3D on the App Store (com.playmaxgroup.tricksterprank), published by Playmax Group LLC.

CubeFlick is a puzzle game. It is free to play and is supported by ads. This page explains what data the game and its partners collect, why, and the choices you have.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress, stars, hints, coins, unlocked styles and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage (and in your device backups, if you use them). There is no account, sign-in or game server copy. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The game shows ads through AppLovin MAX, which works with these ad networks: Google AdMob and Google Ad Manager, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Liftoff (Vungle), Unity Ads, Pangle, ironSource, Moloco and Digital Turbine (DT Exchange).

  • Full-screen ads may appear between levels and, after you have played a while, when you come back to the app. Never while you are playing a board, and none during the first levels.
  • Rewarded videos are always your choice (for example one more heart, a hint, double coins or free coins in the Shop).
  • To show and measure ads, these partners may collect device and app information, your IP address (approximate location) and how you interact with ads. They act under their own privacy policies.

Tracking and your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: the game asks your permission before it or its partners can use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and you still see ads, only less personalised ones. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game also asks for your consent first (Google's consent form), and Settings → Privacy options in the game lets you change it at any time.
  • Daily puzzle reminders are local notifications created on your device. The game asks before sending any, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

Analytics, crash reports and attribution

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): anonymous gameplay events (for example level started or completed), crash reports and app performance, used to fix problems, balance difficulty and tune how often ads appear.
  • AppsFlyer: measures which advertising campaign brought a player to the game and the revenue from ads. It uses the IDFA only if you allowed tracking; otherwise it uses Apple's privacy-preserving measurement (SKAdNetwork).

No partner receives your name, email, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The game does not sell personal data.

Children

CubeFlick is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.
