CubeFlick privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026. Applies to CubeFlick: 3D Arrow Puzzle (com.marfascollection.cubeflick), published by Marfa's Collection (SMC-Private).

CubeFlick is a puzzle game. It is free to play and is supported by ads. This page explains what data the game and its partners collect, why, and the choices you have.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress, stars, hints, unlocked skins and settings are saved only on your device, in the game's private storage. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them. There is no account, sign-in or cloud copy.

Advertising

The game shows ads through AppLovin MAX, which works with these ad networks: Google AdMob and Google Ad Manager, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Liftoff (Vungle), Unity Ads, Pangle, ironSource, Moloco and Digital Turbine (DT Exchange).

  • Interstitial ads may appear between levels, never while you are playing a board. None appear during the first levels.
  • Rewarded videos are always your choice (for example one more heart or a hint).
  • To show and measure ads, these partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They act under their own privacy policies.
  • Your choices: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first (Google's consent form) and Settings → Privacy options lets you change it at any time. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, which stops personalised ads.

Analytics, crash reports and attribution

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): anonymous gameplay events (for example level started or completed), crash reports and app performance, used to fix problems, balance difficulty and tune how often ads appear.
  • AppsFlyer: measures which advertising campaign brought a player to the game and the revenue from ads, using the advertising ID and device information.

No partner receives your name, email, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The game does not sell personal data.

Children

CubeFlick is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.
